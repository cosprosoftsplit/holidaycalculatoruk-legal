Frequently Asked Questions

How do I calculate my holiday entitlement?

Enter your work pattern (full-time, part-time, or irregular hours) and the App calculates your statutory holiday entitlement in days or hours.

Does it include bank holidays?

Yes. You can choose whether to include or exclude the 8 UK bank holidays in your entitlement calculation.

Can I calculate pro-rata entitlement?

Yes. If you started or left employment mid-year, the App calculates your pro-rata holiday entitlement.

Does it handle different work patterns?

Yes. The App supports full-time, part-time, compressed hours, and irregular working patterns.

Is my data secure?

Yes. All data stays on your device. Nothing is transmitted to any server.

Is this legally binding?

No. The App provides estimates based on statutory minimums. Your employer may offer more generous holiday terms.

The App is not working properly. What should I do?

If the issue persists, please contact us at

  • Make sure you have the latest version of the App installed
  • Restart the App
  • Restart your device
  • Reinstall the App
